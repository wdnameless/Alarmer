## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Slide-Out Side AI Drawer
The application MUST provide a dedicated slide-out side panel for the AI assistant triggered by a clean side button rather than a tab.

#### Scenario: User clicks side AI toggle
- **WHEN** user clicks the sleek side button
- **THEN** the AI assistant panel smoothly slides out from the side over or beside the main dashboard.

### Requirement: Streamlined Two-Tab Top Navigation
The top tab selector MUST only contain primary views (Dashboard and Settings).

#### Scenario: Top navigation appearance
- **WHEN** user looks at top bar
- **THEN** only "Дашборд" and "Настройки" tabs are displayed.
