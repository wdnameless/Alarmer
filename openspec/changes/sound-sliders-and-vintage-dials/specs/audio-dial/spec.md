## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Audio Sliders & Dropdown Voice Selector
The settings MUST present voice options as a dropdown selector, provide independent volume sliders for clicks and alarms, and instantly audition voices upon selection.

#### Scenario: User adjusts click volume
- **WHEN** user drags the click volume slider
- **THEN** click sounds scale in loudness accordingly.

### Requirement: Vintage & Multi-Style Interactive Dial
The timer dial MUST support visual style presets including Vintage Clock and Chronograph with animated tick transitions and fluid drag-to-set.

#### Scenario: User selects vintage style
- **WHEN** vintage style is chosen
- **THEN** Roman numerals, vintage tick hands, and pendulum tick sound are active.
