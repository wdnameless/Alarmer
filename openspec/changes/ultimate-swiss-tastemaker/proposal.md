# Proposal: Ultimate Swiss Tastemaker Alarmer

## Why
Building the definitive, high-craft, minimalist smart alarm and workout assistant:
1. **Visual Language (Tastemaker / Braun Dieter Rams)**: Deep matte graphite background, precision 1px markers, typographic hierarchy (Inter/Helvetica), clean 12/3/6/9 analog indications, seamless hairline progress ring.
2. **Architecture**: Two-module expanding physical window. Left 340px remains pure, clean, dedicated dial. Right 340px opens seamlessly as a deep command wing for complex routines, AI prompt decomposition, and settings.
3. **Sound Philosophy**: Pure acoustic escapement mechanical clock tick (FLAC) + tactile frequency beeps.
4. **AI Orchestration**: Takes unstructured multi-day workout texts and generates an interactive, editable preview card (schedule accordion) before one-click deployment into alarms.
5. **Data Sovereignty**: Local offline Tauri store with direct JSON Export and Import.
